Swim Team

Deep 25 m lanes · six days a week · Masters mornings

The swim team trains in the deep-end 25-meter lanes: weekday afternoons from 3:30 (until 7:00 on Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 8:00 on Tuesday and Thursday), Saturday 7:30–11:00 and Sunday 11:00–1:00. Masters swims 6:00–7:00am, Monday to Saturday.

Questions for the Meadowbrook team

Everything here was transcribed from what Meadowbrook publishes. These are the places where the schedule goes quiet or where we had to make a call — each one is also written on the calendar entry itself.

  • GapIs the February schedule still the fall schedule?
    The lane schedule is a PDF named for February 19, 2026, with no dates printed on it. The calendar runs it from the week after. If the swim team or lesson times changed for the fall, the new ones go in from the day they start, and the winter ones stay behind them.
  • GapAre the white cells open lap swim?
    Most of the grid is empty. Members presumably swim laps there, but the PDF doesn't say so, and the calendar doesn't book lanes nobody named. Say the word and every open lane shows as lap swim, lane by lane.
  • ConflictWhen does recreational swim run?
    The Meadowbrook page says “Recreational swim begins at 10AM”. The lane schedule shows Rec Swim only on Saturday and Sunday, 2–6pm, in the Therapy Pool.
  • OursAqua Fitness on lanes 1–2
    Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 9–11am, Aqua Fitness fills lanes 1–2 at both the deep and the shallow end. We entered that as one class using both ends.
  • GapMasters in 50 m lane 3
    On Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday, Masters also takes 50 m lane 3. Is that a long-course group, and does it have its own coach or sign-up?

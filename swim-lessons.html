Swim Lessons

Group and private lessons · therapy pool and shallow lanes

Lessons run most of the day in the Therapy Pool, weekday afternoons in shallow lane S1 (and S2 from 5:30), weekend mornings, and in deep lanes D1–D2 on Monday, Wednesday and Friday evenings.

Live from Pool Relay · open the full calendar →  —  Each block shows the pool and the lanes the lesson uses.

Which water is my child in?

On the lane schedule, lessons are a yellow cell in whichever of four grids they fall on. A parent looking for a 5:30 lesson has to check the shallow lanes and the Therapy Pool page to find it.

Here every lesson block says where it is: Therapy Pool, shallow lane S1, or deep lanes D1–D2 — so a parent knows where to stand before they arrive.
